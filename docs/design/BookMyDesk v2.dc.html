BookMyDesk · v2

Visual pass on the current repo: Mon–Fri only, seats grouped by table, owners Martin Minarovič (B21102) and Jiří Forman (B21101), and the new sign-in copy. Earlier version: v1.

Visual system
A CETIN Blue band with a faint floor-plan grid sits behind each tab header. The content panel overlaps it with a 28px radius. Half-day glyph: a half-filled circle means morning or afternoon, a full one means the whole day. It appears on tiles, the slot toggle, tickets and Who's in. Seat tiles put the big desk number first. The B211 prefix is shown small above it. My bookings are shown as tickets. Today's ticket is blue and carries the check-in. The tab bar floats at the bottom with a frosted-glass background.
